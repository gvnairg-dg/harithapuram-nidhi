Gold loan in one visit

How a gold loan works

Bring your gold & KYC

Ornaments or coins plus Aadhaar and PAN. New customers become members at the same counter.

Appraisal in front of you

Our certified appraiser tests purity and weighs net gold. You see every reading.

Sealed & insured

Ornaments are packed in a tamper-proof pouch you sign, then stored in our strong-room vault.

Cash or bank transfer

Cash up to the legal limit, the rest by NEFT or IMPS to your account — usually within 30 minutes.

A mutual benefit company

Owned by the people it serves

A Nidhi is a company whose members save and borrow among themselves. Every depositor and borrower is a shareholder. The money raised in Alappuzha stays in Alappuzha and is lent back to the fishermen, coir workers, farmers, traders and families who put it there.

We are incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, and file our half-yearly returns with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

Help

Frequently asked questions

What is a Nidhi company?

A Nidhi is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 and governed by the Nidhi Rules, 2014. It exists to build saving habits among its members and lends only to them. It is regulated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

Can anyone deposit money with you?

No. We accept deposits and give loans only to our members. You become a member by buying one equity share of ₹10 with your KYC documents — it takes about ten minutes at any branch.

How much can I get for my gold?

Up to 75% of the market value of the net gold content. Use the gold loan calculator above for an estimate; the branch appraiser confirms the final figure after testing.

Is my gold safe with you?

Your ornaments are sealed in a pouch you sign, stored in a burglary-resistant strong room, and covered by insurance for the whole loan period.

What happens if I can't repay on time?

Talk to your branch before the due date — you can pay the interest and renew. If a loan stays overdue we send written notices before any auction, and any surplus from an auction is returned to you.

Are my deposits insured by DICGC?

No. Deposit insurance by DICGC applies only to banks. Nidhi deposits are not covered, which is one reason we keep lending secured against gold, property and deposits.
